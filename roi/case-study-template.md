## How to use your case studies
- Lead the next pitch with the *closest-fit* case study to that prospect's industry.
- Put the ROI number in the subject line / first slide.
- Stack them: "Here are 3 clients in your space and what we booked them."
- Each new case lets you raise price and tighten the guarantee.
