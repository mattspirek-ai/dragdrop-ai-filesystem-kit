# Proposal Builder

## When to use
The user has discovery notes / call summary and pricing options, and needs a clean,
persuasive proposal.

## Inputs you need
- Discovery notes (the client's problem + desired outcome in their words)
- Scope of what you'll deliver
- Pricing options (ideally 3 tiers)
If pricing tiers aren't provided, structure good/better/best and ask the user to confirm numbers.

## Steps
1. Restate the client's problem and desired outcome (shows you listened).
2. Frame the proposed solution as the bridge between them.
3. Lay out scope as concrete deliverables, not activities.
4. Present 3 investment tiers anchored to outcomes, not hours.
5. End with a single clear next step.

## Output format
```
1. The Problem (their words)
2. Desired Outcome
3. Proposed Solution
4. Scope & Deliverables
5. Investment (3 tiers: what's included + price)
6. Timeline
7. Next Step
```

## Quality bar
- Lead with the client's world, not your company bio.
- Deliverables are outcomes ("a booked-call pipeline"), not tasks ("email setup").
- Tiers make the middle option the obvious choice.

## Example
**Input:** notes from a discovery call + 3 price points.
**Output:** A skimmable 1-page proposal where the recommended tier is clearly framed.
