# LinkedIn Content System

**Promise:** Attract, engage, and build authority on LinkedIn on autopilot — a
consistent stream of on-brand posts that generate inbound conversations.

## Who it's for
Founders, consultants, and B2B teams who know LinkedIn matters but can't post consistently.

## Components used
- **Prompts:** [LinkedIn Post (authority)](../01-prompts/content.md), [Headline Doctor](../01-prompts/content.md), [Viral/Shareable Angle Finder](../01-prompts/marketing.md)
- **Skills:** [content-repurposer](../02-skills/content-repurposer/SKILL.md)
- **Agents:** [Content Agent](../03-agents/content-agent.md)
- **Workflows:** [Topic → Published + Distributed](../04-workflows/topic-to-published-content.md)

## Setup (one-time)
1. **Codify voice** — run the Brand Voice Guide prompt on 3-5 past posts; save the output.
2. **Pick 3 content pillars** — the themes you want to be known for.
3. **Build a 20-idea backlog** — use the Angle Finder against each pillar.
4. **Set the cadence** — e.g., 3 posts/week, 1 repurposed long-form/week.

## Operating cadence (weekly, ~60 min)
1. Pick 3 ideas from the backlog.
2. Content Agent drafts each in your voice; Headline Doctor sharpens hooks.
3. Repurpose your best post of the week into a newsletter blurb + 2 short videos.
4. Reply to every comment for the first 60 min (engagement compounds reach).

## What you get
A repeatable authority engine: consistent posting, a refilling idea backlog, and inbound
conversations instead of cold chasing.
