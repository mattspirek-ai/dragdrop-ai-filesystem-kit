# Content Repurposer

## When to use
The user has one strong source asset and wants a week of multi-channel content from it,
in their voice.

## Inputs you need
- The source asset (article / transcript / notes / video script)
- (Optional) brand voice notes or 1-2 samples to match tone

## Steps
1. Extract the 3-5 distinct ideas worth their own post.
2. For each channel, adapt the idea to that channel's native format and length.
3. Preserve the source's specifics (numbers, examples) — repurposing ≠ vague restating.
4. Keep the user's voice; if voice notes were given, match them.

## Output format
```
Core ideas extracted: 1) 2) 3)
Newsletter blurb: ...
LinkedIn posts (3): each with a distinct hook
Short-video hooks + scripts (3)
Tweets/threads (5)
Carousel outline (1)
```

## Quality bar
- Each output stands alone and is publishable as-is.
- No "as I mentioned in my article" filler — each piece is native to its channel.
- Reuse concrete details; don't water them down.

## Example
**Input:** a 1,500-word article on pricing strategy.
**Output:** 1 newsletter blurb, 3 LinkedIn posts, 3 video hooks+scripts, 5 tweets, 1 carousel — all carrying the article's specific frameworks.
