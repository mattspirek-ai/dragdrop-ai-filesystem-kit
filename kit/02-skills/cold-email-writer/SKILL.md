# Cold Email Writer

## When to use
The user has a specific prospect (or ICP) and an offer, and needs a first-touch email
that earns a reply.

## Inputs you need
- Prospect: role, company type, and any personalization hook (recent post, company news, trigger event)
- The offer + one proof point (result, number, or client)
- Desired CTA (book a call / quick reply / resource)
If the personalization hook is missing, ask for it — generic emails fail.

## Steps
1. Identify the prospect's most likely pain given their role + trigger.
2. Open with a specific, earned observation (never "I hope this finds you well").
3. Connect their pain to the offer in one line, backed by the proof point.
4. Make one clear, low-friction ask.
5. Write 3 subject-line options optimized for open + honesty (no clickbait).

## Output format
```
Subject options:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

Email (under 90 words):
[body]
```

## Quality bar
- Under 90 words. One ask. No jargon, no "synergy", no "just checking in".
- The first sentence must reference something true about THIS prospect.
- Sound like a person, not a sequence.

## Example
**Input:** VP Ops at a 200-person logistics firm; just posted about warehouse hiring pain. Offer: AI scheduling that cut a client's overtime 22%. CTA: 15-min call.
**Output:** Subject: "warehouse hiring + your overtime line" → 80-word email referencing the post, the 22% result, and a one-line ask for a 15-min call.
