# Lead Gen Prompts

---

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Builder
**Use when:** You're targeting too broadly.
```
You are a demand-gen strategist. Build a tight ICP.

INPUTS:
- What I sell + best 3 past customers (why they were great): [paste]

OUTPUT: Firmographics · Trigger events that signal readiness · Where they hang out · 3 disqualifiers · A one-line ICP statement.
```

---

### Lead Magnet Ideas
**Use when:** You need an opt-in.
```
Generate lead magnets that pre-qualify buyers (not just collect emails).

INPUTS:
- Offer: [paste] · ICP: [paste]

OUTPUT: 7 lead magnet concepts ranked by buying-intent signal, each with title + the exact next step it sets up.
```

---

### Cold Email (first touch)
**Use when:** Starting an outbound sequence.
```
Write a cold email that earns a reply.

INPUTS:
- Who I'm emailing (role + company type): [paste] · Their likely trigger/pain: [paste] · My proof: [paste]

CONSTRAINTS: Under 90 words. No "I hope this finds you well." One clear ask. Subject line = 3 options.
```

---

### Lead Scoring Rubric
**Use when:** Reps waste time on bad leads.
```
Build a lead scoring rubric I can apply consistently.

INPUTS:
- What a great-fit lead looks like: [paste] · Signals I can observe: [paste]

OUTPUT: 5-7 scored criteria with point values, the threshold for "sales-ready," and an auto-disqualify list.
```

---

### Inbound Reply Triage
**Use when:** A form fill / DM comes in.
```
Triage this inbound lead and draft the first response.

INPUTS:
- Their message + any context: [paste]

OUTPUT: Fit (hot/warm/cold) + why · The one question to ask next · A reply draft under 70 words.
```

---

### Referral Ask
**Use when:** A customer is happy.
```
Write a natural referral request.

INPUTS:
- Customer + the result they got: [paste]

OUTPUT: A short message that makes referring easy (names the ideal intro, gives them copy-paste wording to forward).
```
