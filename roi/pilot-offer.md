# The 30-Day Guaranteed Pilot

This is the offer you sell *before* you have case studies. Its whole job is to remove the
client's risk so you can land the first 2-3 clients and generate the data that becomes your
proof.

## The offer
> **"30-day pilot. We book you [N] qualified sales meetings or you get your money back.**
> **Fixed fee of $[X]. No long contract. At the end you'll have the meetings, the data,
> and a clear decision on whether to continue."**

### Recommended structure
- **Fee:** small and fixed — e.g. **$1,500–2,500** for 30 days. Low enough to be an easy yes.
- **Guarantee:** a concrete meeting floor (e.g. "at least 5 qualified meetings or full refund").
  Set the floor *below* your honest projection so you clear it comfortably.
- **Qualified = defined in writing** (see [measurement framework](measurement-framework.md)).
- **Term:** 30 days, then a continue/stop decision. No lock-in.

## Why it works
- **Removes risk:** they're not betting on AI hype; they're betting a small fixed fee against a refund.
- **Forces a baseline:** the pilot setup captures their current numbers — your future ROI proof.
- **Self-selecting:** a business that won't risk $1,500 to test new pipeline isn't a real buyer.
- **Generates the asset you're missing:** real funnel + revenue data → case study #1.

## The economics (run it yourself)
With the kit, your marginal cost to run a pilot is mostly your time + tooling (email infra,
data). So even at a $1,500 pilot fee with a refund risk, your downside is small and your
upside is a $2,000–5,000/mo retainer + a case study worth far more than the pilot fee.

Model any client's numbers:
```
python roi_calculator.py --name "Their Co" --deal 6000 --meetings 8 --close 0.2 --fee 1500 --months 1
```

## Pilot → retainer conversion
At day 30 you present the [ROI report](roi_calculator.py) with actuals and say:
> "Here's what we booked, here's the pipeline it created, here's your ROI. To keep this
> running it's $[retainer]/mo. Want to continue?"

If you hit the numbers, this close writes itself — because it's just math they watched happen.

## First-3-clients plan (your case-study factory)
1. Pick **one** narrow niche (e.g. "B2B SaaS founders selling to HR teams"). Same niche = reusable messaging + compounding proof.
2. Run 2-3 pilots at a discount in exchange for a testimonial + permission to publish results.
3. Convert the winners to retainers; turn each into a [case study](case-study-template.md).
4. Raise your price with each new proof point.
