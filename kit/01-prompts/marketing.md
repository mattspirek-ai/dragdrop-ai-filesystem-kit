# Marketing Prompts

---

### Positioning Statement
**Use when:** Sharpening how you describe the offer.
```
You are a positioning strategist (April Dunford style). Draft a positioning statement.

INPUTS:
- Product: [paste] · Best-fit customer: [paste] · Top alternative they'd use instead: [paste] · Unique value: [paste]

OUTPUT: For [customer] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternative], because [proof]. Then 3 alternate angles.
```

---

### Landing Page Copy
**Use when:** Building or rewriting a landing page.
```
Write conversion-focused landing page copy.

INPUTS:
- Offer + price: [paste] · Audience: [paste] · Top 3 benefits: [paste] · Proof (testimonials/numbers): [paste]

OUTPUT: Hero headline (5 options) · Subhead · 3 benefit blocks (header + 2 lines) · Objection-busting FAQ (5) · Primary CTA (3 options).
```

---

### Audience Positioning Map
**Use when:** You serve multiple segments.
```
Map how to position my offer for different segments.

INPUTS:
- Offer: [paste] · Segments: [list 2-4]

OUTPUT: For each segment — their language for the problem, the benefit that lands, the proof they trust, and a one-line hook.
```

---

### Customer Retention Play
**Use when:** Churn or low repeat purchase.
```
You are a lifecycle marketer. Design a retention play.

INPUTS:
- Product + typical lifecycle: [paste] · Where churn happens: [paste]

OUTPUT: The single highest-leverage retention moment, the message/offer for it, and how to measure if it worked.
```

---

### Viral/Shareable Angle Finder
**Use when:** You want content that spreads.
```
Generate shareable content angles for my topic.

INPUTS:
- Topic/offer: [paste] · Audience: [paste]

OUTPUT: 10 angles, each tagged with the emotion/identity it triggers and the best format (thread, short video, carousel).
```

---

### Brand Voice Guide (from samples)
**Use when:** You need consistency across writers/tools.
```
Reverse-engineer my brand voice from samples and codify it.

INPUTS:
- 3-5 writing samples: [paste]

OUTPUT: Voice in 5 adjectives · Do/Don't list · 3 signature phrases · A "rewrite this boring sentence" before/after example.
```

---

### Campaign Brief
**Use when:** Kicking off any campaign.
```
Write a one-page campaign brief.

INPUTS:
- Goal + metric: [paste] · Audience: [paste] · Offer: [paste] · Channels available: [paste]

OUTPUT: Objective · Single-minded message · Audience insight · Channel plan · Hero asset · Success metric · Timeline.
```
