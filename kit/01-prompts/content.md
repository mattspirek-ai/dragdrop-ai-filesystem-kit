# Content Prompts

---

### Long-Form Article (from outline)
**Use when:** Turning a brief into a draft.
```
You are an expert writer in my niche. Draft a long-form article from this brief.

INPUTS:
- Brief/outline: [paste] · Brand voice notes: [paste] · Audience: [paste]

CONSTRAINTS: Lead with the most useful insight. Concrete examples over fluff. No "in today's fast-paced world."
OUTPUT: Full draft with H2/H3s + a 1-line TL;DR at top.
```

---

### LinkedIn Post (authority)
**Use when:** Building authority on LinkedIn.
```
Write a LinkedIn post that teaches one idea and invites discussion.

INPUTS:
- The idea / lesson: [paste] · A real example or story: [paste]

CONSTRAINTS: Strong first line (the scroll-stopper). Short lines. One takeaway. End with a question. No hashtag spam.
OUTPUT: 3 variations with different hooks.
```

---

### Repurpose One Asset Into Ten
**Use when:** You have one good piece of content.
```
Repurpose this into a week of multi-channel content.

INPUTS:
- Source asset (article/transcript/notes): [paste]

OUTPUT: 1 newsletter blurb · 3 LinkedIn posts · 3 short-video hooks+scripts · 5 tweet/threads · 1 carousel outline. Keep each in my voice.
```

---

### Newsletter Issue
**Use when:** Writing a recurring email.
```
Draft a newsletter issue my audience actually opens.

INPUTS:
- Theme/lesson this issue: [paste] · Audience: [paste] · CTA: [paste]

OUTPUT: Subject line (5) · Preview text · 250-400 word body with one clear idea · CTA.
```

---

### Video / Short Script
**Use when:** Making short-form video.
```
Write a 30-45 second short-form video script.

INPUTS:
- Topic + the one point: [paste]

OUTPUT: Hook (first 3 sec) · Body (problem → insight → proof) · CTA. Include on-screen text cues.
```

---

### Headline Doctor
**Use when:** Your headline is weak.
```
Improve this headline and explain why.

INPUTS:
- Draft headline + what the piece is about: [paste]

OUTPUT: 7 stronger headlines across angles (curiosity, benefit, contrarian, specific-number) + the one you'd ship and why.
```
